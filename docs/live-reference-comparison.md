# Live Reference Fidelity Audit

## Homepage

The live homepage uses a large, image-led hero with a sunset cityscape, transparent top navigation, and the headline positioned low-left. Its public navigation labels are **Products**, **About**, **Services**, **Consulting**, **FAQ**, and **Car Sales — SOON**. The source includes a Client Stories section below the four-step process; this project must not fabricate testimonial content, so that section will remain omitted unless the owner supplies verified testimonials.

## Property Catalogue

The source catalogue uses a dark navy hero and a compact, labelled filter strip. It exposes purpose chips, type chips, and three selects: location, fixed price range, and sort. Its visible labels are `Listing`, `Type`, `Location`, `Price range`, and `Sort`. The recreated catalogue currently uses a different free-form search and min/max-price treatment; the filter structure, labels, and sorting need correction.

## About

The live About hero is a two-column navy panel with company copy on the left and a modern-house image on the right. Its supporting copy, figure treatment, and leadership image selections differ from the current recreation. The source uses 477+ properties sold and 97% satisfaction in the visible composition, and full-length mission and vision statements that should be restored.

## Services

The live Services hero remains a text-only navy panel. The services are laid out as six lightly bordered cards in a three-column grid with individual line icons, rather than a broad dark-card treatment. Its process section has exact four-step copy and a narrower CTA: “Tell us what you're looking for.” / “Share your brief and a dedicated advisor will build a tailored shortlist for you.”

## FAQ

The live FAQ has three groups of three questions. The final source question is “When will the Car Sales division launch?”, not a vehicle-submission question. Its exact explanatory answers should replace the abbreviated recreation copy.

## Consulting and Contact

The reference contact hero says “book an inspection — physical or virtual.” The request form includes specific explanatory copy, a file upload field accepting PDF, JPG, PNG, or DOC up to 5 MB, and a long example placeholder. The scheduling panel has its own explanatory sentence and labelled inspection type. A real interactive map appears below the forms on the live page and must be restored in the corrected recreation.

## Car Sales

The live Car Sales route is a coming-soon landing page, rather than a vehicle listing catalogue. It has an email input labelled “Your email address”, a “Notify Me” button, and a secondary “Browse Properties” action. The recreated application should retain its real vehicle-listing workflow through the new administration functionality, but restore the source landing-state presentation at the public car route until approved vehicle inventory exists.

## Post-correction verification

The recreated public routes were re-captured at desktop and mobile breakpoints after the corrective pass. The homepage now has the reference’s cinematic, liquid-glass navigation treatment, headline placement, process composition, rounded internal CTA, and footer-card structure. The property catalogue uses the source labels, chips, select controls, and price buckets. Car Sales now presents the source launch state until approved vehicle inventory exists, at which point the controlled vehicle catalogue is shown. Reference navigation, About, Services, FAQ, and Contact wording, composition, scheduler controls, file-drop treatment, and map block were corrected. The source Client Stories section was intentionally not reproduced because this project may not include unverified customer reviews or ratings.
